Chapter 01

It starts on a cold pasture

Grass-fed herds, clean northern air and a short, rich grazing season. Bokkaido is where Tiama's ingredient story quietly begins — and the reason the milk is worth importing at all.

  • Grass-fed herds on open pasture
  • Cold air, short grazing season

Chapter 02

An exceptionally rich milk

Higher in fat and protein than ordinary milk, with a naturally soft, near-vanilla character. It was chosen for what it does to skin — real moisturization, not just fragrance.

  • Higher fat and protein
  • Soft, faintly vanilla character

Chapter 03

Brought to India — a first

Tiama became the first brand to bring Bokkaido milk into a soap bar, importing the real ingredient rather than settling for a synthetic stand-in. Milled and finished in India.

  • The real milk, flown in
  • Milled and finished in India

Chapter 04

One base, three moods

The same milk base is finished in three fruit-inspired moods — Blueberry, Goji Berry and Acai. The fruit names the mood and sets the colour and scent; the milk is what does the work.

  • Colour and fragrance change together
  • Fruit-inspired — not fruit-filled

Chapter 05

And the bar takes shape

Milled and pressed into the oval bar you'll actually hold — rounded to sit in the hand, in two sizes, with the same milk story inside every colour.

Before you pick

Does the bar actually contain fruit?

No. The three moods are fruit-inspired — the fruit names the mood and sets the colour and fragrance. The moisturizing ingredient is the imported Bokkaido milk.

What makes Bokkaido milk different?

It is higher in fat and protein than ordinary milk, with a naturally soft, near-vanilla character — which is what makes it useful in a moisturizing bar rather than just a pleasant smell.

Which mood should I start with?

Blueberry if you shower in the morning, Goji Berry for slow evenings, Acai if you want the richest, most indulgent version. At ₹49 a trial bar, starting with the wrong one costs very little.
